SKYSTONES RULES

Goal: Control more stones than the opponent when the 3x3 board is full.

On your turn: Pick a stone from your hand, rotate it, then place it on an empty board cell.

Capture: When you place a stone next to an enemy stone, your blades are compared. If your blade count is higher, you capture their stone. Elements add +1 to your count if you have the advantage.

No Chain Reactions: Captured stones do not capture their own neighbors. Only the stone currently being played can capture stones.

Abilities: Each Skylander has one unique power usable once per battle.
