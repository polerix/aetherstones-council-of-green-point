RULES OF AETHERSTONES

Goal: Control more tiles than the Oracle when the board is full.

On your turn: Pick a tile from your hand, rotate it (CCW/CW), then click an empty cell to place it.

Capture: When you place a tile next to an enemy tile, your edge is compared to theirs. If yours is higher (with element modifier), you take their tile. Your tile stays rotated as placed.

Chain Reaction: Captured tiles immediately check all THEIR neighbors — captures cascade in a single placement!

Element Advantage: 🔥 beats 🌿, 🌿 beats 🌬️, 🌬️ beats 💧, 💧 beats 🔥. Advantage gives +1 to your edge comparison.

Ability: Each councilor has one unique power, usable once per battle. Use it wisely.
